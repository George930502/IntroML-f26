## Overview

This course provides a broad introduction to machine learning: the principles, algorithms, and applications of ML from the point of view of formulating learning problems, representation, clustering, and classification. Topics covered include

- Introduction and basic concepts
- Regression
- Dimensionality reduction and clustering
- KNN and Bayesian classifiers
- Decision trees and ensemble methods
- Linear classifiers, perceptron, and SVM
- Neural networks and deep neural networks
- Convolutional neural networks
- Recurrent neural networks and Transformers
- Large language models and generative models
- Reinforcement learning and trustworthy ML
- Model selection and evaluation
